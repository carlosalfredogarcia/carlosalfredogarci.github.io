About

Hi, I'm María Luisa

A visual artist, illustrator and designer originally from Valencia, Venezuela, and now based in Fuerteventura, Canary Islands. Colour is at the heart of my work. Through painting, illustration and design, I love exploring the beauty of nature, the stories behind everyday things and the places that become part of who we are.

Honorable mention (accésit) in the 1st and 2nd Open-Air Painting Contests of Puerto del Rosario · Author of Lucas y Martín (2024).
